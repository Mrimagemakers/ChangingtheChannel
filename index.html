Golden Rule #2 • Pause Before You Play

4-6 Cadence & 90-Second Reset

"The pause is where your power lives. Emotional chemistry surges quickly, but a two-breath pause interrupts the automatic loop."

READY 01:30
Instant Grounding Checklist (Rules #2 & #7):
  • Breathe in for 4 counts, exhale slowly for 6 counts.
  • Put your phone down before answering any triggering text or email.
  • Say aloud: "I need a minute to think about that."
  • Count backward from 10, uncross your shoulders, and ground both feet.

Bonus Feature 2 • Emotional Hygiene

Daily Channel Changer Exercises

"Emotional intelligence is not a checklist you master once. It is a daily rhythm you return to."

Morning Reset (5 Minutes) Set Your Channel

1. What am I feeling as I begin today? • 2. What do I need most today? • 3. What channel do I want to lead with (peace, courage, clarity, patience)?

Midday Check-In Reacting vs. Responding

1. What has affected my mood so far? • 2. Am I reacting or responding? • 3. What one physical action (Rule #7) would help me reset?

Evening Reflection Review & Renew

1. What emotion showed up most today? • 2. What did I handle well today? • 3. What will I do differently tomorrow?

Bonus Feature 3: 7 Guided Journaling Prompts
  1. What emotion do I avoid most, and why?
  2. What story have I outgrown but still replay?
  3. Where in my life do I need clearer boundaries?
  4. What triggers me most quickly, and what does that reveal?
  5. What would emotional maturity look like in my relationships?
  6. What does self-compassion sound like in my own voice?
  7. What kind of emotional atmosphere do I want to create around me?
